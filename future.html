The Future

Looking ahead at my goals and how computer science connects to my future.

My Future in Computer Science & Beyond

In this computer science class, I learned a lot of useful skills that I can use in the future. I improved my problem-solving, coding abilities, and learned how to fix mistakes when my code does not work. These skills can also help me in other subjects, especially math and science, because they require critical thinking and focus.

One of my goals related to computer science is to continue learning more about coding and technology. Even if I do not pursue a career in computer science, I know these skills will still be useful because technology is used in almost every field.

During this class, I learned that I am capable of figuring things out if I keep trying and do not give up. At first, some assignments were confusing, but over time I became more confident with coding and problem-solving. I also learned that I enjoy projects where I can be creative and build something from scratch.

One weakness I still have is debugging code when there are a lot of errors at once. Sometimes it takes me a while to figure out what is wrong. To improve this, I can keep practicing, ask for help when needed, and learn from my mistakes instead of getting frustrated.

I also want to work on managing my time better so I can stay more organized and finish projects earlier instead of rushing at the end.

My Career Goal

In the future, I want to become an obstetrician. This career interests me because I want to help people and be involved in caring for mothers and babies. I know it takes a lot of hard work, responsibility, and science knowledge.

Computer science connects to my future goals because it has helped me build patience, problem-solving skills, and attention to detail. These skills will be useful in any medical career because doctors also need to think critically and solve problems under pressure.

Overall, this class helped me grow not just in coding, but also in confidence and persistence, which I will carry with me into the future.
